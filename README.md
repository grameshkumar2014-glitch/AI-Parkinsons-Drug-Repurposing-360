# AI Parkinson's Drug Repurposing 360

## Evidence-Based Drug Repurposing Research Framework

**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** Infrastructure Development

AI Parkinson's Drug Repurposing 360 is an independent research
framework for identifying, evaluating, and prioritizing potential
drug-repurposing candidates for Parkinson's disease.

The framework integrates:

- Biological mechanisms
- Target validation
- Binding and target engagement
- Preclinical efficacy
- Human clinical evidence
- ADMET and safety
- Biomarkers
- Patient stratification
- Clinical trial intelligence
- Failure analysis
- Biology deficits
- Information gain
- Reproducible candidate ranking

## Scientific Boundary

This project is intended for research prioritization and hypothesis
generation.

Candidate ranking does not constitute a clinical recommendation,
proof of efficacy, regulatory approval, or evidence of individual
patient benefit.
